🌈 Learnscape Adventure

Explore. Discover. Learn Shapes!

Welcome to Learnscape Adventure, a fun and interactive educational web game designed to help young learners, especially kindergarten students, recognize and learn different geometric shapes through exciting adventures!

Join Bibi, your friendly rabbit guide, as you explore colorful islands, complete missions, discover hidden shapes, and unlock rewards. Every island offers a new adventure where learning becomes a fun experience!

🎮 How to Play

Choose an Island – Begin your adventure on Shape Island Map and explore the available levels.

Learn a Shape – Meet a new geometric shape and discover its characteristics.

Complete Missions – Search for and identify shapes using your observation skills.

Use Your Camera – Point your camera at a shape and let the game detect and recognize it.

Get Helpful Hints – Receive guidance and encouragement from Bibi whenever you need help.

Earn Rewards – Complete your missions, unlock treasure chests, and collect badges.

Explore More Islands – Progress through the levels and discover more shapes!

✨ Game Features

Interactive Learning – Learn geometric shapes through missions and activities.

Camera-Based Shape Recognition – Use your device's camera to detect supported shapes.

Colorful Adventure Map – Explore a world of unique islands designed for young learners.

Friendly Guide – Get help and encouragement from Bibi, your rabbit companion.

Rewards and Achievements – Unlock treasure chests and earn badges as you progress.

Kid-Friendly Design – Enjoy simple instructions, colorful visuals, and engaging gameplay.

Progressive Challenges – Practice identifying different shapes as you advance through the game.

👧🧒 Who Can Play?

Learnscape Adventure is designed primarily for kindergarten students and young children who are beginning to learn geometric shapes. Parents, teachers, and guardians can also use the game as a supplementary learning activity.

💻 How to Play the Web Game

Open the game using its published website link.

Use a computer, laptop, or compatible device with a supported web browser.

Allow camera access when prompted to use the shape recognition feature.

Follow the on-screen instructions and start your adventure!

Tip: For the best camera detection experience, use good lighting, keep the shape clearly visible, and hold it at a suitable distance from the camera.

🎯 Our Goal

Learnscape Adventure aims to make learning geometric shapes more enjoyable, interactive, and engaging by combining educational activities, game-based rewards, and camera-based shape recognition.
