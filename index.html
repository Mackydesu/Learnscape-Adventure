    Privacy Policy

    Effective date: 10/02/2026
    Operator: Pangasinan State University
    Privacy contact: narcisomarkgil@gmail.com / 09913314291

    Learnscape Adventure is a shape-learning game for children and families.

    Information stored on your device

    Learnscape stores game information in your browser on the device you use. This may include your chosen player’s name, optional avatar image, frame color, sound settings, game progress, badges, stars, carrots, and game notifications.

    This information helps the game remember your progress. It is not sent to our server by the current version of Learnscape.

    You can remove this information by using the game’s Clear My Data control, when available, or by clearing Learnscape website data in your browser settings.

    Camera Challenge and object detection

    Learnscape asks for camera access only after you choose Start Camera.

    In the current version, Learnscape displays a live camera preview for the Camera Challenge. We do not record, save, upload, transmit, or share camera photos, video, or audio. Camera access ends when you leave the Camera Challenge, finish it, close the game, or revoke camera permission in your device settings.

    The current version does not use facial recognition, biometric identification, cloud object detection, or automated decisions about a child.

    If we later introduce real object detection, we will update this policy and show a clear notice before that feature is used. We will explain whether processing occurs on-device or through a service provider, what information is processed, why it is needed, how long it is retained, and what choices parents or guardians have.

    Children’s privacy

    Learnscape is designed to minimize data collection. Children should ask a parent, guardian, or teacher before using the camera or choosing an avatar image.

    We do not ask children to provide contact details, address, phone number, school, precise location, or social-media account information to play the game.

    Parents or guardians may contact us at narcisomarkgil@gmail.com or 09913314291 to ask questions, request access to information we hold, request deletion, or withdraw permission for future processing.

    Sharing

    We do not sell children’s information. We do not use targeted advertising or behavioral profiling.

    The current website loads fonts from Google Fonts. This may cause the browser to connect to Google’s services. We will identify any future service providers here before using them with personal information.

    Security and retention

    We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information we process. Local game data remains on the device until the player clears it, browser data is cleared, or the browser removes it. We retain server-side data only as long as necessary for the stated purpose, if server-side features are added.

    Your rights and contact

    Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, object to, or complain about the use of personal information. Contact narcisomarkgil@gmail.com or 09913314291 first. Philippine users may also contact the National Privacy Commission.

    Changes

    If we materially change how Learnscape handles personal information, especially camera or child data, we will provide notice before the new processing begins.

    Questions about these Terms or Privacy Policy can be sent to narcisomarkgil@gmail.com or contact 09913314291.
